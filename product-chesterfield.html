Chesterfield - Specifications

A chesterfield sofa is the easiest to spot. It has a luxurious look with the back and arm fitted with deep button tufts. The rolled arms are the same height as the back. The front of the arms is usually nail-head trim which gives it a visual gravity. It has the flair to be your living room’s showstopper.
