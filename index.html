    Our Specialities & Services

    We provide Furnishing Cloths, Curtain Cloth, Rexine, U-Foam, Mattress, Sofa Set Materials and everything related to Design & furnishing.

    All types of Sofa Making

    We make all kinds of sofas and provide all sofa materials like hygienic clothes, waterproof clothes, sofa nets, sofa covers, etc. We also offer various sofa cushions and blocks, like back cushions and custom-made cushions.

    All types of Curtain Making

    We make all kinds of curtains and provide fitting options. We have vertical blinds, zebra blinds, roller blinds, hospital blinds, Roman & Venetian blinds, remote-controlled blinds, etc. We also have a variety of pipes, rods and channels.

    All types of Carpet, Wallpaper & Net Fitting

    Carpet: Wooden flooring, PVC flooring, Tiles flooring, Acrylic and non-woven flooring. We also provide wallpaper & Net fittings, such as Mosquito nets, roller nets, shutter nets, sliding nets, etc.

    All types of Blanket, Quilt, Mat & Comforter provider

    We provide high-quality Blankets from Valenci, Raymond, Boccassa, Landmark & Signature, to name a few. We have a unique lightweight Jaipuri quilt which weighs only 100 grams. We also have all kinds of mats, like Door mats, dining table mats, bath mats, yoga mats, etc.

    All types of Mattress, Pillows, Cushions & Blocks provider

    We have all types of mattresses, such as Memory Foam, Spring, High-density Foam, HR foam with nifty gel tech, & Binder Foam for back support. We also have vacuum, Memory, gel, Allergy-free, Curved, Counter, & Musical pillows, and all kinds of blocks & cushions.

    All types of Furnishing Materials provider

    We also provide Bubble wrap, Packing materials, thermocol, epe foam, bedsheets, mattress toppers, waterproof toppers, bath rugs, toilet sets, Towel sets, shower Curtain, PVC curtains, Cushion covers, Rexine-Cotton fabrics, High-quality Leather Effect rexine, Car furnishing, Freeze mat, Jaipuri sheets, Slimpack stool & many more..
