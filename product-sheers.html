Sheers - Specifications

Thin, semi-transparent fabrics are the basis of sheer curtains. They let in the most daylight when closed, but give the least privacy and are mainly decorative.
Sheer curtains are most popular in living rooms and dining rooms.
Some decorators add a liner, so you can enjoy the beauty of the sheers while gaining added privacy and extra features.
