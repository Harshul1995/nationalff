Jaipuri razai - Specifications

Jaipuri Razai, or the Quilt of Jaipur, is made with one of the most time consuming and slow method of printing called the Hand Block Printing. However, this method is capable of yielding highly artistic results with mesmerizing designs which are unobtainable by any other method.
Filled with export quality cotton batting and fine hand quilted, the Jaipuri Razai becomes an instant favorite in the bedroom, spreading its grace all over the place
