Crystal rods - Specifications

Crystal clear curtain rods are just transparent, nothing special. This hardware is preferred as it is not visible from a far distance these rods are great for homeowners who do not wish to use curtain valances to conceal the rod. Crystal clear rods can be spring tension, sash-rods, or any other type.
